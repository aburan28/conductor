# Conductor live experience audit — 2026-11-13

This is the state observed on the owner's Mac on Nov 13 (Pacific time), not a
release claim. The live daemon was initially `v0.1.11-local-retention` and was
then provisionally updated with the locally validated status-query change from
[PR #140](https://github.com/aburan28/conductor/pull/140). The CLI was also
provisionally updated from [PR #141](https://github.com/aburan28/conductor/pull/141);
the app remains v0.1.11. None of PRs #138–#141 is described here as merged or released.

   verify the NAS copy once the NFS share is mounted.

Local validation for the versioned operational files: `bash -n`, `shellcheck`,
`plutil -lint` on a rendered plist, `make check`, and `make ci`. Live S3 and
restore receipts above are separate from those source checks.

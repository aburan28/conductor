Conductor @VERSION@

Conductor keeps your coding agents from colliding: it knows who is editing what, and tells the next agent before it walks into someone else's work.

This installs Conductor.app in Applications, for @ARCH_SENTENCE@, and links the conductor, conductord and conductor-mcp commands into /usr/local/bin.

Open Conductor afterwards: it starts its control plane and a private database on this Mac, signs you in, and walks you through connecting your coding tools.
